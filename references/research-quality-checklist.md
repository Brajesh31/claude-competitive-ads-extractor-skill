# Competitive Ads Research Quality Checklist

## Scope
- [ ] Competitors, platforms, geography, language, and date range are defined.
- [ ] Research questions and intended deliverable are clear.

## Collection
- [ ] Each record has a source URL and collection date.
- [ ] Only public or authorized sources were used.
- [ ] Platform terms, access controls, and rate limits were respected.
- [ ] Private personal data and sensitive-trait inferences were excluded.

## Analysis
- [ ] Observable facts are separated from interpretation.
- [ ] Repeated appearances are not treated as proof of performance.
- [ ] Performance claims are supported by reliable performance data.
- [ ] Missing metadata and archive limitations are disclosed.

## Recommendations
- [ ] Campaign ideas are original and differentiated.
- [ ] Competitor creative is not copied.
- [ ] Each hypothesis is testable and tied to observed evidence.
