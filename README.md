# Competitive Ads Extractor

A research skill for analyzing publicly available competitor advertising and turning observations into campaign insights.

## Capabilities

- Research ads in public platform libraries and authorized sources.
- Compare messaging, positioning, offers, and creative formats.
- Classify ads by audience context and customer problem.
- Identify recurring themes and changes over time.
- Prepare evidence-based summaries and original campaign hypotheses.

## Usage

Use for competitor ad research, positioning analysis, and campaign planning. Record source links and collection dates. Distinguish observable facts from interpretation.

## Legal and ethical use

Respect platform terms, copyright, trademarks, and privacy requirements. Do not bypass access controls or copy competitor creative. Public ad examples are research inputs, not proof of campaign performance.
